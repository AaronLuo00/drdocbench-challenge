Why this challenge

Expert-Level Document Parsing

Dr.DocBench is a difficulty-aware benchmark for expert-level document parsing, built from roughly 4,500 annotated pages across 52 subject domains. It targets difficult content such as chemistry notation, dense tables, mathematical formulas, irregular layouts, and complex reading order.

Evaluation

Evaluation Framework

The Document Parsing track uses the Dr.DocBench composite Overall metric. Additional evaluation details are TBA.

Document Parsing metrics
Text Edit Distance
Measures text-recognition accuracy; lower is better.
Table TEDS
Measures table structure and content similarity; higher is better.
Formula CDM
Measures formula recognition with character detection matching; higher is better.
Reading Order
Measures the recovered sequence of document elements; higher is better.
