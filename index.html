Shared task at DocInsights 2026

Dr.DocBench Challenge

Expert-level document parsing under real difficulty

Co-located with EMNLP 2026 - Budapest, Hungary

Two tracks evaluate structured document extraction and long-document robustness on difficult, expert-domain content where modern parsing systems still struggle.

Why this challenge

Strong benchmark scores do not guarantee expert-level document understanding.

Dr.DocBench is a difficulty-aware benchmark built around pages where strong document parsers disagree or fail. It covers dense tables, mathematical notation, music, reference works, technical manuals, multilingual content, irregular layouts, and cross-page structures.

4,514annotated pages
312long documents
52BISAC domains
14written languages
70K+fine-grained annotations
Challenge objective. Move document parsing evaluation beyond easy OCR wins toward robust structure recovery, expert notation, and long-document consistency.

Challenge scope

Two complementary tracks

Teams may participate in either or both tracks. Each track has an independent leaderboard and award pool.

Track 1

Document Parsing (Overall)

End-to-end structured extraction from difficult document pages, including text, tables, formulas, layout, and reading order.

  • Per-document structured predictions
  • Expert-domain and multilingual content
  • Composite evaluation across parsing components
Official track
Track 2

Long-Document Robustness

Tests whether systems preserve reading order and document structure as the usable context expands from individual pages to long document windows.

  • Context windows from 1 to 15 pages
  • Reading-order robustness under scale
  • Cross-page structure and continuation handling
Official track

Optional spotlight

Sheet-music transcription

Music notation remains an open failure mode for current systems. A non-ranked spotlight may be offered for MusicXML transcription without changing the two official leaderboards.

Not part of official ranking

Benchmark data

Difficulty-aware, subject-diverse, and structurally rich

Dr.DocBench concentrates evaluation on cases that expose current parsing limitations rather than uniformly sampling easy pages.

01

Expert-domain content

Chemistry, medicine, law, design, games, reference works, music, and technical subjects introduce notation and layouts absent from ordinary OCR datasets.

02

Complex structures

Dense and borderless tables, formulas, rotated text, multi-column reading order, cross-page continuation, and hierarchical layout are explicitly represented.

03

Fine-grained supervision

Annotations support layout, recognition, reading order, and domain-specific structures for both overall evaluation and diagnostic analysis.

Evaluation

Transparent component metrics, final ranking details to follow

The evaluation dimensions are confirmed. The precise Overall aggregation for Track 1 and final ranking aggregation for Track 2 will be published with the evaluation kit.

Track 1 metrics
Text Edit Distance
Normalized Levenshtein distance; lower is better.
Table TEDS
Structure and content similarity for parsed tables; higher is better.
Formula CDM
Character detection matching for mathematical formulas; higher is better.
Reading Order
Sequence accuracy derived from normalized edit distance.
Overall formula: TBA with the evaluation release.
Track 2 protocol
Context scale
Evaluation across document windows ranging from 1 to 15 pages.
Primary capability
Reading-order and structural consistency as context grows.
Diagnostic reporting
Performance curves across context-window sizes.
Final ranking
Aggregation rule will be released before submissions open.

How it works

Participation flow

The challenge follows the proven release, validation, private evaluation, and workshop-results workflow used by the organizing team.

  1. 1

    Download the release

    Review sample data, identifiers, submission examples, and the evaluation protocol.

  2. 2

    Validate predictions

    Submit to the public validation phase and resolve schema or alignment errors.

  3. 3

    Run final evaluation

    Select final submissions for evaluation on the private held-out test set.

  4. 4

    Publish the leaderboard

    Final rankings are determined independently for the two official tracks.

  5. 5

    Present at DocInsights

    Top teams provide system descriptions and share findings with the workshop community.

Participation policy

Rules and team requirements

Eligibility

Open to research and industry teams worldwide. Final team-size and affiliation rules will be confirmed before submissions open.

External data

Teams must disclose external training data, proprietary APIs, ensembles, and any additional model resources used.

System descriptions

Top-ranked teams will be asked to provide a concise technical description sufficient to understand and reproduce the approach.

Workshop participation

Top teams may be invited to present their systems and diagnostic findings during the DocInsights workshop.

Submission limits

Daily limits and the number of final submissions will be announced with the EvalAI configuration.

Final authority

The released evaluation specification and platform timestamps will be the systems of record for challenge decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What participants should know now

Can a team enter both tracks?

Yes. The tracks have independent leaderboards and awards, and teams may participate in either or both.

Are the evaluation formulas final?

The evaluation dimensions are confirmed, but the Track 1 Overall formula and Track 2 ranking aggregation will be released with the evaluation kit.

When will data and submissions open?

The exact sample-release and submission dates are being aligned with the workshop schedule and will be announced here.

Is sheet-music transcription a ranked track?

No. It is currently planned only as an optional, non-ranked spotlight outside the two official tracks.

Will there be a public validation leaderboard?

Yes. The planned platform flow includes public validation and a private held-out final evaluation.

Where should questions be sent?

Email the DocInsights workshop chairs. Platform-specific support details will be added when EvalAI opens.
