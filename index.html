Why this challenge

Expert-Level Document Parsing

The Dr.DocBench Challenge focuses on expert-level document parsing at the intersection of document recognition, layout understanding, and structured extraction. Based on Dr.DocBench, it uses a dedicated subset of around 1,000 pages selected around cases where strong document parsers struggle.

Document Parsing

Expert-Level Document Parsing

Participants convert difficult document images into structured Markdown while preserving:

  • Original-language text and document structure
  • Structured HTML tables and LaTeX formulas
  • Reading order, chemistry notation, and musical notation

Evaluation

Evaluation Framework

Submissions will be evaluated using document-parsing metrics aligned with Dr.DocBench. The proposed metrics are shown below; the final metric set and aggregation formula will be published with the evaluator.

Document Parsing metrics
Text Edit Distance
Measures text-recognition accuracy; lower is better.
Table TEDS
Measures table structure and content similarity; higher is better.
Formula CDM
Measures mathematical-formula recognition; higher is better.
Reading Order
Measures the recovered sequence of document elements; higher is better.
Overall
Combines the confirmed evaluation metrics to determine the ranking.
Overall formula: TBA with the evaluation release.

How it works

Participation flow

The challenge follows a public development, validation, held-out evaluation, and workshop-results workflow.

  1. 1

    Data release

    Public development data will be released through Hugging Face.

  2. 2

    EvalAI submission

    Teams upload structured predictions on EvalAI.

  3. 3

    Public validation

    EvalAI will provide a public validation leaderboard.

  4. 4

    Held-out evaluation

    Final rankings will be determined on held-out data.

  5. 5

    Results workshop

    Results will be announced at DocInsights 2026.
